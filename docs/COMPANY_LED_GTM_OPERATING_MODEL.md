# Traceli company-led GTM operating model

Updated: 1 September 2026

This document defines who publishes, who replies, who conducts commercial conversations and where human accountability sits. It replaces any default assumption that Traceli requires a founder-influencer motion.

The commercial objective remains unchanged: convert qualified donor-funded procurement conversations into a paid six-week Award Assurance Pilot using the action `Test one completed tender`.

## Channel decision

Traceli is the public publisher. The company should sound like a precise institutional research and assurance practice, not a personal-brand content engine.

| Function | Public identity | Permitted activity | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Publishing | **Traceli** | Publish official-source case notes, checklists, product explanations, FAQs and pilot material | No invented individual author or unsupported institutional claim |
| Editorial byline | **Traceli Research Desk** | Sign company research, source notes, evidence newsletters and methodological material | A team byline under Traceli, not a person, employee or separate legal entity |
| General inbound and coordination | **Traceli Partnerships** | Answer general enquiries, coordinate introductions and route qualified prospects | Use a functional Traceli-controlled mailbox; do not invent a staff biography |
| Founder accountability | **Odey, Founder of Traceli** | Join qualified calls, approve claims, explain the company, negotiate scope and make commercial commitments | No public posting quota or founder-influencer requirement |
| Specialist review | Named real reviewer or `Traceli review` | Review factual, legal, communications, security or procurement material within their actual authority | Never imply external review or credentials that have not been obtained |

A dummy identity is not a public cofounder, employee, profile, byline or sender. A test account may be retained only for internal testing and must never contact external people or appear in public metadata. A real future team member may be represented only with their consent, accurate role and verifiable credentials.

## Personal-profile boundary

Odey's personal profile may remain accurate and quiet. It is a verification surface, not the primary publishing channel.

- Keep the founder role, biography and verifiable prior experience accurate.
- Do not impose a personal content calendar, engagement quota, manufactured opinion or founder-story format.
- Do not operate the profile through a proxy, fictional persona or undisclosed automated activity.
- Use the profile selectively for accepting relevant connections, making a private introduction or confirming that Odey is the accountable founder when this materially helps a qualified conversation.
- Publish from the Traceli company Page and owned evidence library by default.

LinkedIn permits company Page administrators to publish text, documents, images, video and scheduled posts as the Page, and supports newsletters authored by a Page. Its professional-community rules require real identities and accurate qualifications. See [Post as your LinkedIn Page](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1660869), [Create a LinkedIn Page newsletter](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a596833) and [LinkedIn Professional Community Policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies).

## Distribution architecture

1. **Owned evidence library:** the canonical article, case page, source note, checklist or control template lives on a Traceli-controlled surface.
2. **Traceli company Page:** publish one useful derivative that stands on its own and links to the canonical source.
3. **Before You Sign newsletter:** publish a monthly synthesis from the Traceli Page after the platform and approval gates are verified.
4. **Email and known-network distribution:** send the relevant asset to DIHAD contacts, practitioners and partners with a specific reason for the recipient to care.
5. **Practitioner channels:** prioritize roundtables, working groups, guest resources and partner distribution over mass-social reach.
6. **Qualified conversion:** move a prospect to the 30-minute fit call only when the content or conversation reveals a plausible control moment, accountable owner and proof gap.

The public channel earns recognition and trust. It does not replace direct follow-up, qualification or the founder's presence in a consequential sales conversation.

## Editorial cadence

- One source-led company Page post each week when an approved source supports it.
- One substantial LinkedIn document or evidence card every two weeks.
- One `Before You Sign` Page newsletter each month after newsletter access and destination readiness are verified.
- One weekly email brief only when it contains new approved evidence or a useful control artifact.
- One monthly practitioner roundtable, partner pitch or closed evidence discussion.
- No posting merely to fill a slot. No default X cadence and no founder-video requirement.

Each asset should do one job: document a consequence, teach a control, answer an objection or invite a completed-tender diagnostic.

## Reply and escalation model

| Incoming signal | Default responder | Required treatment | Escalate when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General comment or source question | Traceli company Page or Research Desk | Answer from the controlled source and link it | The source is disputed, changed or outside the approved register |
| General product or pilot question | Traceli Partnerships | Use approved category, pilot and data-boundary language | The reply becomes account-specific or requests a commitment |
| Named organization, tender or consequence | Odey or designated real commercial owner | Move to a private qualified conversation; request no sensitive files | Pricing, scope, security, procurement, legal or contracting is raised |
| Media, donor-policy or institutional partnership request | Odey plus the relevant real reviewer | Confirm purpose, attribution and claim status before responding | Public representation, endorsement, policy adoption or named-institution claims are involved |
| Complaint, correction or challenge | Odey plus factual and legal or communications review as required | Pause derivative distribution and inspect the source record | A published claim may be materially wrong or misleading |

Warm outreach should preserve relationship truth. If Odey personally met a DIHAD contact, the follow-up may be signed by Odey without requiring any public posting. A general inbound reply may come from Traceli Partnerships. Never write as if a team member attended a conversation they did not attend.

## AI-assistance boundary

AI may help research candidate sources, draft controlled derivatives, classify inbound themes, prepare replies and maintain the editorial run log. It does not become a human cofounder or autonomous public representative.

- Every public asset retains a named human approver and the source/claim gates already defined by Traceli.
- Account-specific replies, pricing, scope, data handling, legal or security commitments require human review before sending.
- No automated connection requests, unsolicited messages, platform engagement or reply loops are authorized by this model.
- Do not create synthetic credentials, personal history, quotations, customer stories or profile images.
- Record whether an output is approved, scheduled, published, corrected or withdrawn. A generated draft is not a published company position.

## Measures

Channel performance is judged by qualified movement, not the visibility of a founder profile.

- intended-role replies that name a real consequence;
- completed tenders named for diagnosis;
- control moment, accountable owner and proof gap identified;
- relevant introductions to procurement, assurance, finance or donor stakeholders;
- newsletter subscribers and evidence-library visits from target organizations;
- fit calls completed;
- paid-pilot proposals and signed or dated contracting paths.

Impressions, follower growth and reactions remain secondary. Report them only beside target-account engagement and buying movement.

## Initial rollout

1. Verify the Traceli company Page, admins, brand fields and destination links.
2. Configure `Traceli Research Desk` as the standard research byline and `Traceli Partnerships` as the general-response signature.
3. Use a Traceli-controlled functional mailbox for general inbound. Keep any dummy identity internal-only or retire it.
4. Adapt the first approved article into one company Page document, one email brief and one source card. Do not create a founder derivative by default.
5. Verify Page newsletter eligibility before scheduling `Before You Sign`.
6. Test every public CTA and reply escalation path before the first distribution cycle.
7. Review results after four weeks using qualified conversations and completed-tender movement, then adjust cadence without changing the category or pilot offer.
