### Objective

[Customer] will use Traceli to reconstruct one completed procurement from its governing rules through supplier evidence, reviewer judgments, clarifications, exceptions, approvals, and award record. The pilot will test whether a customer-selected independent reviewer can explain why the supplier won without rebuilding the decision from disconnected systems, folders, and email.

The customer's original procurement record and decision remain authoritative. Traceli organizes, links, tests, and reports the available evidence; it does not certify compliance, make a retrospective compliance determination, or alter the award.

## Scope

The fixed pilot scope includes:

- one customer organization and one completed tender;
- one approved institutional policy and/or donor-conditions pack;
- the tender, evaluation criteria or matrix, and relevant source versions;
- three to five supplier submissions;
- two to four historical reviewers or decision actors;
- the material clarification, exception, conflict, approval, and award artifacts available for the selected case;
- one independent-review session and one executive results readout.

Any additional tender, submission volume, translation, document remediation, travel, integration, live decision workflow, or customer-specific export is subject to written change control.

## Deliverables

1. **Baseline and control map** covering the governing rules, evidence expectations, systems, actors, and current reconstruction workflow.
2. **Traceli Award Assurance Pack** connecting the material requirements, criteria, evidence, clarifications, judgments, exceptions, approvals, and chronology, or marking them explicitly unresolved.
3. **Independent-review session** using the reconstructed record.
4. **Pilot results brief** showing measured reconstruction performance, accepted evidence gaps, control observations, and recommended next action.
5. **Customer export** consisting of the available human-readable decision pack and machine-readable manifest supported by the pilot release.

## Acceptance measures

The parties will record current baselines during Week 1 and review the following at closeout:

- time required to reconstruct and inspect sampled award evidence;
- proportion of sampled material claims supported by an accepted source or explicit unresolved gap;
- missing approvals, declarations, source versions, clarifications, or other control evidence surfaced;
- time required to produce the review pack;
- whether the independent reviewer can explain the award recommendation from the reconstructed record;
- customer disposition of every reported gap and its decision to stop, repeat, or expand.

The pilot does not guarantee that the historical record is complete or compliant. Surfacing an unresolved or missing record is a valid assurance result, not a delivery failure.

### Value measurement schedule

The parties will agree the available baseline, measurement method, denominator, and evidence owner during Week 1. Where the customer can support them, the results brief will keep the following value layers separate:

1. **Operating value:** people and hours required to assemble, inspect, and explain the award record before and with Traceli.
2. **Cycle value:** evidence questions, review loops, and time through a comparable approval, no-objection, audit, or closeout step.
3. **External-cost value:** documented audit-support, consulting, or remediation work attributable to the award-record problem.
4. **Risk scenario:** customer-provided incident frequency, severity, and attribution assumptions, clearly labelled as a scenario rather than a measured pilot result.

The pilot fee is the only fixed economic input in the standard proposal. Traceli will not infer a customer labour rate, award volume, avoided loss, risk-reduction percentage, or payback period from public cases. Any customer-specific business case will show its inputs and remain subject to customer validation.

## Traceli responsibilities and boundaries

Traceli will:

- enforce the agreed organization, user, document, retention, and export boundary;
- preserve source identity, versions, actors, and chronology available in the supplied record;
- distinguish system-organized evidence from customer-confirmed judgments;
- keep consequential conclusions human-owned;
- export or delete customer pilot data according to the agreed terms at pilot exit.

Traceli will not autonomously score suppliers, exclude a bidder, approve an exception, certify legal or donor compliance, replace the customer's source system, or make an award recommendation.

## Data and security gate

No sensitive customer or supplier document will enter Traceli until the parties agree, at minimum:

- NDA and applicable processing terms;
- data classification, permitted users, region, and approved transfer route;
- relevant subprocessors and model-provider policy;
- retention, deletion, legal-hold, incident-notification, and pilot-exit requirements;
- the authoritative customer system and the boundaries of the copied pilot record.

If the gate is not complete, the parties may conduct scope and control-mapping sessions using non-sensitive descriptions or irreversibly sanitized artifacts, but the six-week delivery clock does not begin.
