# Traceli go-to-market messaging

Updated: 27 August 2026

This document turns the Traceli category and must-have thesis into reusable marketing and sales language. Product truth and claim boundaries remain governed by [BRAND_SYSTEM.md](./BRAND_SYSTEM.md); public consequence examples remain governed by [PUBLIC_CASE_STUDY_REGISTER.md](./PUBLIC_CASE_STUDY_REGISTER.md).

## Message hierarchy

**Category**

> Award assurance for donor-funded procurement.

**Primary promise**

> Prove the award before you sign it.

**Executive outcome**

> Protect funding eligibility. Keep awards moving.

**Product explanation**

> Traceli shows whether every material award decision is connected to the governing rule, supplier evidence, human judgment and required approval—before donor review, complaint or audit.

**Named artifact**

> Traceli Award Assurance Pack.

**Pilot call to action**

> Bring one completed tender.

## Spoken versions

### Ten seconds

“Traceli is award assurance for donor-funded procurement. It helps the accountable team prove the award before signing it.”

### Thirty seconds

“Your tender portal runs the procedure and your ERP records the commitment, but the reason an award is defensible is usually scattered across donor rules, supplier files, reviewer judgments, clarifications and approvals. Traceli connects that proof, exposes what is missing and produces one Award Assurance Pack before review, complaint or audit.”

### Pilot version

“Bring one completed tender. In six weeks, we reconstruct its rules-to-award evidence chain and test whether an independent reviewer can defend the decision without rebuilding it from folders and email. If the result is useful, the next phase places the same gate before one live award.”

## The must-have logic

Traceli does not become necessary by describing more features. It becomes necessary when it is attached to an unavoidable control moment and produces an artifact an accountable executive needs.

| Existing obligation | Control moment | Traceli artifact | Accountable owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Donor or grant conditions | Before material award approval | Control profile plus cited evidence status | Programme director, grants/compliance lead |
| Procurement policy and delegated authority | Before committee or executive sign-off | Approval and exception record | Head of procurement, COO, country director |
| Audit or corrective-action plan | Before the next sampled procurement or closeout | Award Assurance Pack | Internal audit, risk or assurance owner |
| Supplier complaint or integrity concern | Before response or escalation | Source-linked chronology and human dispositions | Procurement, legal, investigations |
| ERP or portal transformation | Before the new workflow becomes authoritative | Cross-system assurance control | CFO, transformation lead, IT and procurement |

Do not ask the prospect to invent a `procurement assurance software` budget line. Find the owner of the existing consequence and contract one defined assurance outcome from programme assurance, audit remediation, internal controls, procurement improvement or technical assistance. If no owner, consequence or budget route exists, the account is not currently qualified.

## Persona emphasis

| Audience | Lead with | Avoid leading with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head of procurement | Fewer last-minute evidence hunts; visible gaps before committee sign-off | AI extraction or integrations |
| CFO / COO / programme director | Funding eligibility, award delay and executive accountability | Detailed document features |
| Grants / compliance | Donor conditions connected to the actual award record | Generic compliance automation |
| Internal audit / assurance | A testable operating record assembled before audit; audit independence preserved | Claims that Traceli replaces audit |
| IT / security | A bounded layer beside existing systems, tenant isolation and controlled evidence custody | A rip-and-replace platform story |

## Campaign territories

Use one territory at a time so the category remains memorable.

1. **Before you sign** — the pre-award proof gate.
   - Hook: `The award is ready only when its proof is.`
   - CTA: `Bring one completed tender.`
2. **The proof behind the award** — the rule-to-evidence golden thread.
   - Hook: `Can your team answer why this supplier won without opening five systems?`
   - CTA: `Watch the award-proof story.`
3. **The consequences do not stay inside procurement** — public institutional evidence.
   - Hook: `A missing decision record can become an audit, funding or integrity problem.`
   - CTA: `Inspect the evidence library.`
4. **Your ERP records the commitment. Traceli proves the decision.**
   - Hook: direct answer to the `ERP feature` objection.
   - CTA: `See the control model.`

## Claim guardrails

Use:

- `award assurance`, `award readiness`, `source-linked proof`, `configured control profile`;
- `designed to surface earlier`, `supports`, `connects`, `preserves`;
- the exact procedural language and amounts in the public evidence register.

Do not use:

- `certifies compliance`, `guarantees audit success`, `fraud-proof`, `prevents corruption`;
- `funding was suspended` when the source says an investigator recommended preventing future undue spending;
- `trusted by` for team experience marks or public-source institutions;
- `Vanta for procurement` in public category language. Internally, the useful analogy is the operating model: defined controls, continuous evidence, a shareable assurance artifact and a recurring gate.

## Conversion measurement

For the twelve calls, measure message quality by buying movement:

- named completed tender;
- named consequence and trigger date;
- named accountable sponsor;
- named existing budget route;
- proposal requested or accepted;
- security, legal or procurement owner introduced;
- decision date agreed.

Clicks, film views and positive reactions are supporting signals. They do not replace movement toward a paid pilot.
