The app was delivered.
The fallout lasted years.

ArriveCAN's estimated C$59.5 million cost was only part of the story. In 2024, Canada's Auditor General could not establish the precise total from the records.

Then came the burden of answering for it:

224 calendar days: the average response time for ArriveCAN-related information requests in 2024-25.

Seven years: the federal contracting exclusion imposed on supplier GC Strategies in a separate integrity decision in 2025.

Public criticism. Experts pulled into responses. Future business closed off.

This is the exposure procurement leaders recognize: a decision that keeps demanding explanations long after the work has been delivered.

Earlier evidence checks could have exposed gaps and reduced the scramble to answer later.

The peace of mind is knowing why you chose the supplier, what supported the price and where the evidence is, before those questions become public.
