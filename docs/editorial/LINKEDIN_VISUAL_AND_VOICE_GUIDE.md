# Traceli LinkedIn: editorial visuals and voice

Effective: 9 September 2026. Applies to Traceli company LinkedIn images, document carousels and accompanying post copy.

## Direction and authority

Odey requested that future LinkedIn branding and display move closer to how [@maybeworthbuilding](https://www.instagram.com/maybeworthbuilding/) publishes, particularly its hooks, text and highlights. This direction supplements the company [brand system](../BRAND_SYSTEM.md) for this channel. It does not rename Traceli or change the website's design system.

The reference was inspected in the public Instagram cover grid on 9 September. Visible covers combine large editorial images or screenshots, dark backgrounds, tightly stacked bold uppercase hooks and yellow blocks behind selected words. Individual posts required login, so interior carousel structure, captions, engagement performance and font identity were not verified. The recommendations below are Traceli's adaptation, not claims about unseen reference content.

## Visual rules

- Use portrait 1080 x 1350 pages for document carousels and primary image posts. Keep all essential text at least 64 px from the edge.
- Lead with an original editorial image, a permitted source crop, or an accurate evidence diagram. An image must carry meaning. Label illustrative or staged material; never present it as a photograph of a real case or customer.
- Use deep Traceli navy `#061a33`, warm white `#fbf5ea` and muted text `#b1bcc8`. Add editorial yellow `#f5dc69` behind the decisive phrase. Yellow here means emphasis, not a risk status. Mint `#78d8c4` continues to identify trace nodes and source links.
- Use IBM Plex Sans Bold for headlines, with deliberate line breaks and tight leading. Use Regular or Medium for supporting text. Keep the trace-node identity and a small Traceli wordmark visible.
- Headlines: normally 80-103 px, 2-4 short lines, one clear thought. Key figures may be larger when their meaning and procedural status remain nearby.
- Highlight one phrase, generally no more than two adjacent lines. The highlight must identify the point of the slide, not compete with several other callouts.
- Body: normally 34-43 px. Essential qualifications: at least 29-31 px. Source/footer metadata may be smaller. Never shrink a dense page until it fits; split the page.
- Build a consistent hierarchy: quiet series label, strong headline, one supporting explanation, necessary qualification, readable source link and progress cue.
- Keep diagrams simple and keep human judgment visible. Do not imply an automated evidence-to-award decision.
- Use page numbers and an explicit continuation cue. Avoid decorative dashboards, rounded-card grids and tiny paragraphs on every page.

## Writing rules

The first two lines should establish a specific decision, consequence or question. Write for procurement and assurance professionals. Be direct without accusing the reader or overstating the source.

Useful hook structures, adapted to the facts of each story:

| Structure | Traceli example |
| --- | --- |
| Decision + unanswered question | The award was signed. Can someone show why this supplier won? |
| Reviewer test | Could a reviewer follow the decision without calling your team? |
| Concrete number + exact meaning | €112m in future funding. OLAF recommended protection until remedial measures were in place. |
| Practical action | Start with one award. Ask seven questions of the record. |

Do not reuse these mechanically. Avoid generic openings such as “In today's complex landscape,” fake urgency, vague “game-changing” claims, invented first-person experiences, unsupported prevalence claims and engagement bait. Use sentence case in post captions; uppercase belongs to short graphic headlines. Hashtags are optional and must be specific. Do not add a block of generic hashtags by default.

Post copy should follow this sequence: hook; brief context; reason to swipe; essential claim boundary; a single primary next step. Paragraphs should be short and readable. The carousel should add evidence or a usable tool rather than repeat the whole caption.

## Carousel sequence

1. Cover: a concrete hook, substantial image, short promise of what the reader gets.
2. Explain the operational problem or control question.
3. Give each materially different case or idea its own slide.
4. Turn the analysis into questions the reader can use. Split a checklist when necessary.
5. Close with one scoped action and a route to the article and primary sources.

Choose the page count from the content. Seven questions do not require seven pages. The first redesigned release uses nine pages to keep the four records and the checklist readable.

## Evidence discipline

Preserve attribution, dates and procedural status. Keep an audit finding distinct from an investigative recommendation, ineligible expenditure, a judgment or a settlement-based debarment. Do not turn a recommendation to protect future funding into a claim of suspension or recovery. Never combine amounts from unlike outcomes into a single loss or savings figure.

Public cases are not Traceli customers, endorsements or prevention stories. A missing evidence link is not proof of misconduct. Do not claim that Traceli certifies compliance, replaces audit, makes the award decision or guarantees outcomes. Use the accepted service positioning: procurement-evidence reconstruction and audit-response support, with named human review and a scoped engagement.

## Production and review

The reusable implementation is `scripts/build-linkedin-editorial-v2.py`. The first complete set is `output/social/before-you-sign-01-linkedin-v2/`; the accessible document is `output/pdf/before-you-sign-01-linkedin-v2.pdf`. The builder retains editable text, source hyperlinks, page tags and an alt description for the illustrative cover. This is not a claim of PDF/UA certification.

Before publication, render every page and inspect the cover and the densest pages at phone size. Check exact headline wording, source qualifications, clipping, line breaks, contrast, page sequence, links and company publisher. Keep source imagery and generation prompts with the asset manifest. Preserve previous releases under their existing paths.

Publication approval remains governed by the approvals register and current user instructions. This guide establishes the future visual and voice direction; it does not independently authorize new claims, paid distribution, outreach or profile changes.
