## LinkedIn company Page post

Could someone outside your team reconstruct why this supplier won?

The approval may be recorded while the reasoning sits across evaluation notes, supplier documents, clarifications and email. When an audit or assurance review arrives, the team has to bring those parts together.

Start with one completed award:

- Can each decisive criterion open its governing requirement?
- Can each material supplier claim open the evidence assessed?
- Can the reviewer, judgment and unresolved questions be identified?

These questions help locate gaps in the record. A missing link does not by itself establish non-compliance or misconduct.

Our article, “The award was signed. The proof was not ready,” examines what a connected award record needs to show.

If your team has a review approaching, tell us the question, the file and the deadline. We can discuss a scoped evidence-reconstruction engagement.
