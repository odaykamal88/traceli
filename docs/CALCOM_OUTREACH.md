## Required intake questions

Keep the form short enough to complete, but require enough information to separate recognition from a current buying case.

1. **What is your organization, role and work email?**
2. **Which best describes your involvement?** Runs or approves procurement; assures, audits or funds procurement; supplies or bids into procurement; other.
3. **Which event creates the need to defend an award?** Donor prior review or no-objection; award approval; supplier complaint; audit or corrective action; programme closeout; internal transformation; no dated event yet.
4. **When is that event expected?** An approximate month is sufficient. Use `No date yet` if unknown.
5. **Can you discuss one completed tender at a high level?** Ask only for procurement type and approximate year. Do not request a tender file, supplier identity, bid value or active evaluation detail.
6. **What part of the award record is hardest to prove today?** Governing rule; supplier evidence; clarification; reviewer judgment; exception; approval; chronology; not yet known.
7. **Who owns the consequence if the record is incomplete or late?** Role or function only; a person's name is optional.
8. **Which function could own a paid pilot if the fit is confirmed?** Programme assurance; audit remediation; internal controls; procurement improvement; fiduciary strengthening; technical assistance; other; unknown. This answer does not establish budget eligibility.
9. **How did you hear about Traceli?** DIHAD conversation; DIHAD referral; website; LinkedIn; direct introduction; other.

Add this warning immediately above any free-text answer:

> **Confidentiality:** Please provide high-level context only. Do not paste supplier names, bid content, evaluation notes, personal data, active-tender information, confidential donor material or links to restricted files.

Do not enable a file-upload question for this event.

## Qualification routing

Review the intake before the call, then confirm the facts verbally. A form response is an initial signal, not account truth.

| Priority | Minimum evidence | Call posture | Required next movement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Priority 1 — external gate** | A donor, funder, regulator or complainant creates a live gate before award, payment, closeout or response; a completed tender and accountable owner are plausible | Keep the call. Diagnose the proof gap and current reconstruction baseline, then test the six-week paid pilot | Named tender, executive sponsor, data owner, customer-approved budget route and dated proposal/SOW review |
| **Priority 2 — dated internal gate** | An executive, committee, audit-remediation plan or transformation programme creates a dated gate; ownership or purchasing route may still be incomplete | Keep the call if the accountable owner and one completed tender can be tested. Focus on the single missing qualification condition | A dated introduction to the accountable owner, tender confirmation, baseline owner or permitted purchasing-route decision |
| **Priority 3 — general concern** | Governance, reputation or future-audit interest without a date, accountable owner or specific proof gap | Do not treat the booking as qualified demand. Use the call only when it can identify a concrete tender and trigger; otherwise move the contact to low-frequency nurture or seek a better-fit referral | A new named gate, owner and proof gap before re-entering an active sales cycle |

Supplier-only contacts are not direct-pilot opportunities under the current ICP. Treat them as a possible referral or future partner only; do not reshape the buyer-side offer during this sequence.

### Thirty-minute call allocation

1. **Reconnect — 3 minutes:** restate the specific DIHAD issue the contact recognized.
2. **Diagnose one completed award — 10 minutes:** rules, systems, missing proof, trigger and accountable owner.
3. **Baseline the work — 4 minutes:** people, hours or days, review loops and documented external support.
4. **Show one relevant proof path — up to 4 minutes:** only if the tender and gap are clear.
5. **Test the paid pilot — 6 minutes:** scope, independent reviewer, data gate, budget owner and contracting path.
6. **Close — 3 minutes:** named next action, owner and date.

Do not open with a general walkthrough. Positive feedback is not a sales outcome.

## Source and campaign tracking

Use one canonical base URL:

```text
https://cal.com/oqemal/30min
```

For links placed in owned outreach, standard URL parameters may be appended for source attribution. Example:

```text
https://cal.com/oqemal/30min?utm_source=dihad&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=founding_pilot&utm_content=followup_01
```

Use a stable lowercase convention:

- `utm_source`: `dihad`, `linkedin`, `website`, `referral`;
- `utm_medium`: `email`, `dm`, `whatsapp`, `cta`;
- `utm_campaign`: `founding_pilot`;
- `utm_content`: a non-personal message or placement code such as `followup_01`, `hero_primary` or `footer_primary`.

Do not put a person's name, email address, organization-sensitive fact or tender identifier into a URL parameter.

Do not assume the scheduling service will store, expose or export URL parameters. Before relying on them, test the approved scheduling page end to end and verify what appears in the booking record and any downstream system. Until that is verified, maintain the authoritative source in the account tracker and retain the required `How did you hear about Traceli?` answer. If the approved base URL already contains a query string, append additional parameters with `&` rather than a second `?`.

## Operating guardrails

- Keep the current offer fixed: one completed tender, six weeks, retrospective analysis and customer-authoritative decisions.
- Describe possible budget homes as discovery routes only. The customer must approve the permitted accounting and purchasing route.
- Do not claim Traceli certifies compliance, prevents corruption, guarantees audit success or would have prevented a public failure case.
- Do not collect sensitive tender artifacts during outreach or scheduling.
- Do not infer ROI from public cases. Baseline the customer's people, time, review loops and documented costs, then measure the pilot.
- Do not count a scheduled call as qualified demand until the control gate, accountable owner and proof gap are confirmed.
