# Traceli public consequence evidence register

Updated: 27 August 2026

This register governs the public institutional examples used to explain why award assurance matters. These are not Traceli customer stories. They show documented consequence patterns; they do not prove that Traceli would have prevented an event.

## Publication standard

Every public claim must have an issuing-institution primary source, a precise procedural label, an owner, an approved paraphrase and a review date. Writers must distinguish:

- allegation from audit observation;
- investigative finding from recommendation;
- recommendation from implemented recovery or suspension;
- debarment or judgment from a general accusation of corruption.

Do not name an individual unless necessary to explain an official final decision. Do not reproduce sensitive operational detail. Do not imply that the institution is a customer, prospect, partner or endorser. A legal/comms reviewer must approve any claim that moves beyond the short wording below.

## Required long-form case-study structure

Each case study should fit on one page and use this order:

1. **What the official record says** — institution, date, procedural status and direct source.
2. **What failed** — the documented control, evidence or decision-trace gap, without speculation.
3. **What followed** — the documented audit, financial, funding or integrity consequence.
4. **The award-assurance question** — the question an accountable buyer should be able to answer before sign-off.
5. **Where Traceli fits** — the rule-to-evidence control Traceli is designed to support, expressly not a prevention claim.
6. **Boundary** — not a customer, endorsement, certification or legal conclusion.

## Recommended ten-day case-study authority sprint

The sprint should produce a credible initial library, not attempt the impossible task of cataloguing every procurement failure worldwide.

### Deliverables

- A research backlog of at least 25 official records from UN oversight bodies, multilateral development banks, the European Commission/OLAF and national aid or development auditors.
- Twelve fully sourced register entries: three auditability, three financial-eligibility, three funding-continuity and three integrity-exposure cases.
- Four reviewed one-page case studies, one from each consequence family.
- Four landing-page excerpts, a four-slide sales proof module and a call-ready objection sheet.
- A reusable claim-review checklist and quarterly refresh owner.

### Work plan

| Days | Work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Lock selection criteria, consequence taxonomy and prohibited wording; build a 25-record source backlog. | Every candidate has an issuing-institution source and procedural status. |
| 3–5 | Extract the twelve strongest cases into the register and map each to one Traceli control question. | No secondary-source-only claim; all amounts and dates checked twice. |
| 6–7 | Draft four one-page narratives and the sales proof module. | Each piece separates the official finding, consequence, inference and product relevance. |
| 8 | Factual, legal/comms and brand review. | Reviewer, date and approved wording recorded. |
| 9 | Publish the approved library and add links from the relevant documentation pages. | Public pages carry source and non-customer disclaimers. |
| 10 | Test on the twelve DIHAD follow-up calls and capture which consequence creates urgency. | Each call records the resonant consequence, objection, owner and next action. |

### Sprint gate

The sprint is complete when twelve entries meet the source standard, four long-form cases pass review, and at least six relevant follow-up conversations produce a recorded response to the consequence narrative. Page views are not the primary validation metric; movement to a named completed tender, sponsor, budget route or proposal is.

## Maintenance

- Re-check live links and procedural status quarterly.
- Archive rather than silently rewrite old approved wording.
- Add later institutional actions when an investigation or recommendation becomes a final recovery, suspension, judgment or closure.
- Remove a public excerpt immediately if its source disappears or the approved wording becomes materially misleading.
