# Traceli affiliation-mark register

Updated: 26 August 2026

This register tracks whether an organization mark may appear in Traceli's `Built by the people from` biography strip. It is a governance record, not evidence of a customer, partner, endorsement, or product relationship.

Do not commit employment records, private correspondence, personal identifiers, or written approvals to this public repository. Store sensitive evidence in an access-controlled location and record only the reviewer, decision, date, and secure reference here.

## Decision rule

A mark passes only when all of the following are recorded:

1. A named team member confirms the genuine relationship and its accurate description.
2. A reviewer records the current mark-use policy and approved asset source.
3. Written permission is securely retained when the policy requires it.
4. The public wording and non-endorsement qualifier match [BRAND_SYSTEM.md](./BRAND_SYSTEM.md).
5. The decision, reviewer, and review date are recorded here.

If any condition is unresolved, the safe fallback is plain text without the affected mark.
