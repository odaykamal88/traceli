## 12 September addition — connect outbound and content

**Source and scope:** Oday shared [Levi Munneke's post](https://x.com/levikmunneke/status/2098441612057887109) on 12 September. Its visible text advocates combining outbound and content, presents cold outreach as a way to learn selling, and argues that advertising skills become more relevant when scaling. The attached clip required login and was not watched. These are the author's views, not evidence of Traceli demand or a universal rule about advertising. The following adaptations are our judgment.

Keep the twelve core tasks and their targets. Apply these additions inside the existing preparation, conversation and review work; do not create a separate content campaign.

Include the asset URL/version and approval status, or an explicit gap. |
| 05, 07, 08 / live 32, 34, 35 — Oday | Keep the first ask easy to answer from experience. Use the proof asset when it genuinely helps a recipient or they request it; reading content is not a prerequisite to replying. Capture exact buyer questions, objections and descriptions of their workaround alongside each response. | Actual message, asset shared or none, send time, response wording, whether the response was prompted by our content, and next step in the existing contact record. |

Distinguish no reply from an objection, and confirmed content engagement from an assumed view. | A brief evidence section identifying the strongest question to answer, source interactions and one next adjustment. Ten contacts across different relationship routes are directional evidence, not a statistically reliable hook test. |

**Working feedback cycle:** relevant outreach → actual buyer language and questions → one useful content answer → use that answer in appropriate later conversations → record what changed. If no substantive replies arrive, there is no buyer-language signal yet: retain the access/delivery/ask diagnosis and mark any content angle as a hypothesis.

**Future condition:** When the same question or objection recurs across independent relevant conversations, reuse the existing editorial workflow to build a small library of answers. Each entry should link the audience and buying stage to a source-qualified explanation, an example and a next step. A single urgent qualified buyer request can justify a tailored answer sooner. Preserve private conversation details; public use needs the applicable permission or an independently sourced/generalized treatment.

Use TB-01/fal.ai only where a visual or short motion makes one of those answers clearer. Keep advertising and bulk outbound deferred until a defined audience, offer and path to qualified conversations show repeatable results and a bounded test has a success measure and budget. Do not treat impressions, automated sending volume or a tool stack as evidence of commercial progress. Reconsider these conditions in Friday's existing decision task; no additional schedule is created.
