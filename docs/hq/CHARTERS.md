# Trace-li mandate roster

Charters are visible before execution. All eight begin with Green internal work only; ongoing loops and consequential tools remain disabled.

- [Buyer Intelligence / Scout](charters/buyer-intelligence.md): Discover costly, recurring procurement and accountability problems with identifiable buyers.
- [Evidence Lab / Investigator](charters/evidence-lab.md): Reconstruct accountability cases into defensible source-linked Traces.
- [Distribution / Editor](charters/distribution.md): Turn verified evidence into useful material that buyers naturally forward.
- [Customer Evidence / Listener](charters/customer-evidence.md): Preserve what buyers actually did, said, spent and agreed to do next.
- [Product / Product Steward](charters/product.md): Turn buyer evidence into testable product priorities and narrow experiments.
- [Build / Builder](charters/build.md): Implement approved product and internal automation in the existing Trace-li repository.
- [Risk / Auditor](charters/risk.md): Independently challenge evidence, product claims, permissions and acceptance results.
- [Institutional Memory / Librarian](charters/institutional-memory.md): Keep canonical company knowledge accurate, scoped, dated and recoverable.

Read the [Agent Constitution](AGENT_CONSTITUTION.md) before assigning any job.
