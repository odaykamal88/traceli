## Six material baseline gaps

### 1. Unknown dates and unsupported certainty

**Observed:** `source_versions.effective_at` is `timestamptz NOT NULL` in [migration 0001](../../../../../db/migrations/0001_tenant_safe_control_graph.sql). The Drizzle definition also uses `.notNull()`. Source records have no date-precision, basis or unknown-reason fields. Source creation time and edition label cannot safely fill these gaps.

**Required:** The migration/backfill plan must represent the contract's nullable date fact, including precision and provenance. Do not fill unknown dates with retrieval, publication, ingestion or zero dates. Existing asserted dates need their actual basis checked before conversion. A changed schema does not retroactively prove a date.

### 2. Same-tenant cross-procurement relationships

**Observed:** `requirements_procurement_tenant_fk` and `requirements_source_version_tenant_fk` separately validate organization/procurement and organization/source-version. They do not establish that the version's source document belongs to that procurement. `findings_procurement_tenant_fk` and `findings_requirement_tenant_fk` likewise permit a finding to point at a different procurement's requirement within the same organization. [Evidence reads](../../../../../lib/domain/evidence-reads.ts) detect inconsistent document procurement and finding requirement membership and fail closed; the database does not prohibit creation of those inconsistent links.

**Required:** Enforce procurement/case-compatible relationships through composite foreign keys or a reviewed database equivalent before intake. Carry scope through every version, snapshot, evaluation and disposition link. Test wrong tenant and same-tenant wrong case/procurement independently. Shared public rules need an explicit authorized relation, not an exception that exposes private source records.

### 3. Lock metadata without source/version immutability

**Observed:** [Migration 0002](../../../../../db/migrations/0002_restricted_application_role.sql) grants `SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE` on `source_documents`, `source_versions`, `requirements`, `findings`, `decisions` and related mutable product tables. `locked_at` is only a nullable timestamp. [Migration 0001](../../../../../db/migrations/0001_tenant_safe_control_graph.sql) protects `audit_events` with a `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` trigger, but supplies no equivalent source-version guard. Integer `version` fields identify current revisions without retaining old rows. HQ history has separate mutation guards in [migration 0003](../../../../../db/migrations/0003_hq_operating_memory.sql); those do not automatically protect product records.

**Required:** Accepted source/rule versions, frozen snapshots, evaluations, human dispositions and releases need immutable content under the application role, linked supersession/correction and same-transaction minimal audit evidence. Bind review and human submission to exact hashes/versions. Preserve lawful minimization/deletion behavior for sensitive payloads; append-only history is not indefinite raw-data retention.

### 4. Hash target, serialization and byte custody

**Observed:** [Seed code](../../../../../scripts/db/seed.mjs) sets `content_hash` to SHA-256 of `fictional-rfp-2026-041-${suffix}`. These are synthetic metadata hashes, not retained-document hashes. The [reader](../../../../../lib/domain/evidence-reads.ts) checks the 64-character hex form and clearly labels citations `stored_reference_only`; it explicitly states that source bytes are not persisted. [Jev `jsonHash`](../../../../../lib/hq/jev.ts) hashes `JSON.stringify(value)`, which is sensitive to object key order; its `raw_response_hash` hashes parsed JSON, not exact HTTP response bytes. [Audit event hashing](../../../../../lib/domain/audit-events.ts) uses a separate sorted-key serialization.

**Required:** Record the hash target, algorithm and serialization version; keep original-byte hashes separate from packet/metadata/normalized-output hashes. Verify retained bytes on intake/retrieval and preserve original-language and translation relationships. Never relabel existing synthetic hashes as byte integrity. For unknown custody, retain `not_retained` or `unverified`. Hash identity establishes neither legal authority nor factual truth.

### 5. Authorization is narrower than tenant identity

**Observed:** The baseline provides `ENABLE` plus `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, a `NOBYPASSRLS` application role and organization-scoped policies. [withTenant](../../../../../lib/db/tenant.ts) sets a transaction-local role and organization; its input validation only checks an `org_` prefix. [Workspace access](../../../../../lib/workspace-access.ts) derives the user, organization and role from authentication. The evidence reader's document access recognizes two exact labels and denies unknown labels; its own comment calls this a local proof, not a complete document ACL. [HQ access](../../../../../lib/hq/access.ts) separately checks configured owner, verified identity, organization and current active membership; viewing HQ is explicitly not an approval or database capability.

**Required:** Preserve server-derived tenant/actor identity and recheck current role/resource/action permissions, including source fetch and export. Add reviewed document, recipient, purpose, version and expiry permissions rather than treating a valid organization ID or readable HQ page as authority. Test removed roles, absent sessions, denied source/export access, revocation and restoration. Give new tables equally restrictive policies and grants. No public-global visibility should follow from sharing a source ID.

### 6. Prototype projections and smoke receipts are not production corpus evidence

**Observed:** The finding command writes hardcoded `evidence_coverage` values of `100` or `86` and fixed blocker copy. [The Jev smoke](../../../../../lib/hq/jev-smoke.ts) persists a fixed synthetic reservation/result in `hq_events.evidence`, guarded by a preview/owner gate and a durable one-attempt reservation. This is not a general unit/evaluation persistence workflow. Jev packet `classification` permits `synthetic` or `public`, but a caller's label alone is not proof of permission. `publicJevReceipt` removes `raw_response` while retaining `input_manifest`; that function name is not permission to disclose future private inputs. HQ event IDs and packet references do not establish relational links to product records.

**Required:** Derive completeness only from explicitly defined evidence with denominators and limitations. Preserve the consumed synthetic smoke allowance; a successful connection does not authorize another provider call, customer processing or a performance claim. General evaluation must validate exact immutable references and permissions, store attempt/error/abstention/retry evidence, preserve unknown cost/usage and keep model, policy and human results separate. Use an allowlisted, purpose-specific projection that excludes sensitive inputs; separately record processing, retention, product-improvement, aggregation and publication permissions. No private intake before those policies and scopes are recorded.

## Implementation handoff and verification limits

M07/M09 should turn these requirements into an additive migration/backfill plan and meaningful invariant tests. Prioritize nullable sourced dates, same-case/procurement relationships and version immutability, then the frozen snapshot/evaluation/disposition path and permission-bound redacted export. Backfills must retain uncertainty and provenance; do not invent missing source metadata or silently treat existing prototype rows as reviewed corpus records.

This inspection confirms code and schema definitions only. It does not confirm that migrations are applied to any live database, verify retained source bytes, run tenant/role integration tests, make a provider request or establish production readiness. No database, application, permission, provider allowance or frontend behavior changed in this mapping task.
