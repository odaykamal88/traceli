## Boundaries and next action

- This first bundle covers Saudi central-government instruments and UAE **federal** instruments. No inference extends federal rules to Dubai, Abu Dhabi, other emirates, free zones, state-owned commercial companies or an unidentified buyer.
- Saudi review needs the exact entity, procurement/contract chronology, governing version, method, value and applicable exceptions/overlays. The retained baseline plus selected amendment pages is not a certified consolidation. Decision 1097 concerns articles 88, 111, 114 and 132; reconcile those against the frozen baseline before extracting obligations.
- UAE law article 4 expressly excludes specified categories, including defence/security, certain international obligations, overseas procurement, medicines, partnerships, property and investment/financing activities. This is a locator summary, not a complete exception test. Prior contracts, amendments and renewals require separate transition review against the exact instrument text.
- Preserve Arabic originals. English notes in this inventory are researcher summaries; the retained MOF English regulations are an official-hosted translation with no equivalence acceptance. No translation is substituted for missing original bytes.
