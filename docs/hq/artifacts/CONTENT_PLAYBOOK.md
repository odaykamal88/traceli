# Trace-li source-led content playbook

Internal operating draft · HQ-C01 · Distribution / Editor · v1 · 14 September 2026

**Review state:** submitted for independent Auditor and Supervisor review. External action is not authorized. This playbook prepares manual work. It enables no publishing, messaging or scheduling loop.

## The editorial job

Help an accountable person recognize an evidence problem in a real decision and carry a useful way of examining it into their own work. The case earns the reader's attention. The source record determines what we may say. Trace-li appears when a small review artifact makes the next step concrete.

The distribution hypothesis is:

**Public case → useful Trace → buyer recognizes a task → bounded private Trace → authorized recipient sharing → another concrete task.**

This is a testable path, not an established acquisition engine. A public case subject is not a Trace-li customer or prospect. A reader's recognition is not buying intent. A promised forward is not an observed forward. A request for a private Trace does not authorize receipt of customer files.

Write for procurement, assurance, finance and programme people who must explain a decision to someone else. Start with the decision they need to defend, what the record establishes and where the chain becomes difficult to follow. Do not lead with AI, a list of features or a claim that software would have prevented a public failure.

## Publisher, voice and conversion

Use the existing company-led model. **Traceli** is the publisher. **Traceli Research Desk** is the functional research byline, not a fictional employee. **Traceli Partnerships** is the approved functional coordination identity where configured. The founder joins qualified and consequential conversations. Agent persona names remain internal and confer no public identity or sending authority.

The canonical asset belongs in the existing company evidence library. Company Page posts and documents are derivatives that remain useful on their own and point to the source note. Retain the established conditional cadence: at most one useful source-led Page post per week, a substantial document or evidence card every two weeks, and the monthly `Before You Sign` synthesis only after destination, eligibility and approvals are verified. Email briefs and practitioner distribution use the existing controlled routes when a new approved artifact gives them a purpose. Do not introduce a founder posting requirement or another channel calendar.

Use the repository's calm, precise voice. Write complete sentences, preserve the institution's response and let the human judgment remain visible. No invented dialogue, professional biography or case scene. A fictional walkthrough must say it is fictional at the point of use. Apply the existing navy, ivory, selective yellow and mint editorial language when a visual asset is commissioned. Choose the page count for the narrative, then verify every rendered page at phone size.

Every piece has one primary action suited to its stage:

| Stage | Primary action | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Public case note or company derivative | Read the source-linked case or use the attached review question. Choose one. | Measured reading or use only where observable. |
| Task recognition / qualified conversation | **Test one completed tender.** | A request to explore a scoped retrospective. No approved price, scope or data transfer is implied. |
| Sanitized Trace test | Ask who needs this record next and why. | A named recipient/workflow hypothesis until the action is observed. |
| Intended recipient experience | **Trace another transaction.** | A separate request subject to its own authorization. This CTA remains a product concept until implemented. |

## Source-to-publication handoffs

1. **Scout finds a candidate.** Save the primary source, issuing body, dates, subject, relevant passages and why the case may illuminate a buyer task. Similarity to an account is a research hypothesis.
2. **Investigator constructs the evidence chain.** Establish chronology, rule, material claim, source location, evidence gap, procedural status, consequence and institutional response. State what the public record cannot establish. Search for later resolution before handoff.
3. **Auditor reviews the case packet.** Reopen primary evidence, test the claim locations and review limits. A source packet that is unreviewed or materially disputed cannot enter a named-case editorial job.
4. **Editor drafts the asset and its derivatives.** Write a short spoken story first. Every factual assertion, number and consequential product claim carries a claim ID in the internal packet. Produce all derivative text from the same reviewed version.
5. **Auditor checks the draft.** Check headline, body, chart labels, image, caption, CTA and source note separately. A qualification essential to a headline must appear with that headline. Required factual and legal/communications review under the existing brand gates remains outstanding unless actually obtained.
6. **Supervisor accepts editorial readiness.** Confirm the useful buyer question, current capability boundary, complete correction path and a proportionate next action. Acceptance of a draft grants no external action.
7. **Oday approves the exact external action.** Bind final artifact/version and hash, channel, audience, sender identity, time window and limits. Publication or sending happens only through the authorized executor. No autonomous replies, connection requests or platform engagement.
8. **Listener records actual evidence.** A published URL proves publication. A real reply or consented forwarding record proves that event. Extract only authorized, appropriately redacted facts for mandate handoff. Librarian receives reviewed lessons and corrections, not raw customer correspondence.

Keep existing public-case register IDs when reusing a source. HQ job and asset IDs supplement those IDs. They do not create a competing source of truth. A new article or derivative needs its own review of the actual wording even when an older asset was approved.

## First sequence, with dependencies

Run the first sequence manually within the HQ allocation. Customer discovery continues throughout. The sequence is an order of preparation, not a promise to publish every slot or wait for every slot before a conversation.

| Order / proposed task | Deliverable and accountable mandate | Entry gate | Done means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish product truth | HQ-B01 explainer, Editor → Auditor → Supervisor. | Read current product boundaries. | Accepted copy distinguishes intended Trace/sharing from the fictional local proof and preserves the ERP boundary. |
| 2. Make one useful object | HQ-A/E sanitized Trace work, Product Steward/Builder → Auditor. | Use fictional or authorized sanitized input. No customer discovery pause. | A reviewer can inspect one conclusion, its rule/source links and explicit missing evidence. Unimplemented controls are labelled. |
| 3. Run the sharing question | Product Steward/Listener prepare an authorized conversation test. | Reviewable Trace plus actual conversation/access authorization. | Record the proposed recipient role, purpose, redactions, missing information, objections and dated next action. Mark unknowns and intentions accurately. |
| 4. Tell one reviewed public story | One case article, company Page derivative and source-linked review card, Editor. | Investigator packet passes Auditor review. Reuse a suitable existing registered case when fresh evidence supports it. | Draft packet accepted with sources, claim IDs and CTA. Publication remains a separate Red job. |
| 5. Use the evidence to choose the second story | A distinct broken link and useful task, Editor with Scout/Listener. | Actual first-cycle evidence if available. Otherwise keep ranking explicitly provisional. | A second accepted packet adds a distinct practical lesson. No arbitrary quota overrides the evidence gate. |
| 6. Synthesize only when earned | `Before You Sign` company newsletter or practitioner resource. | Several useful accepted findings, configured destination and exact publication approval. | The synthesis adds a control question or tool. It does not simply repeat posts. |

For workstream D, prepare four account-specific slots: **UNFPA, OCHA, Chemonics and DAI**. Investigator determines each case's defensible distinction from verified public evidence. Do not assign an alleged failure type to an institution to fill a content grid. In the editorial intake, leave `distinct_broken_link: unverified` until the source packet supports it. A distinct slot may concern rule applicability, approval evidence, delivery-to-payment evidence or review chronology, but these are possible analytical lenses, not findings about any named organization. If the records do not support four distinct pieces, return the gap to Supervisor.

## Exact working templates

The Markdown templates below map to the distribution payload in `schemas/output.schema.json`. Final machine-readable packets must use that schema's exact field names and value types. These are preparation templates, not fabricated output records.

### 1. Source intake required from Investigator

```text
case_id: <preserve existing public-case register ID>
case_version: <integer>
related_job_id: <HQ job>
source_packet_review_ref: <independent review record>
subject_and_scope: <organization, process, geography, covered dates>
primary_sources: <issuing body, title, URL, publication date, retrieval time>
chronology: <dated source events, each with claim ID and location>
claim_register:
  <claim ID> | exact supportable statement | source URL | page/paragraph
  | fact/allegation/recommendation/decision/inference | required qualification
  | management response | later resolution / not found | review state
broken_link: <what evidence or relationship the source establishes is absent/weak>
consequence: <source's exact consequence, separate from the broken link>
unknowns: <what the record does not establish>
permitted_reconstruction: <limits of a public or fictional Trace>
distinctness: <difference from other accepted pieces>
next_owner: Distribution, only after source packet review
```

For amounts, capture currency, denominator, period and exact procedural meaning. A recommended recovery is not money recovered. An audit observation is not a conviction. A source documenting several causes does not establish that weak records caused the whole outcome.

### 2. Editorial packet required from Editor

```text
job_id: <HQ job>
mandate_id: distribution
artifact_id: <stable ID>
version: <integer>
classification: internal
produced_at: <ISO timestamp>
sources: <schema-compliant source objects with claim, location and limitations>
assumptions: <explicit creative/product hypotheses>
known_gaps: <missing source, capability or performance evidence>
review_status: review
next_owner: Risk Auditor, then Supervisor
payload:
  audience: <accountable role and concrete review task>
  hook: <a specific sourced decision, consequence or useful question>
  broken_chain: <supported missing or weak relationship>
  reconstruction: <how the reader follows the source evidence>
  consequence: <exact sourced consequence, or explicitly none established>
  takeaway: <one reusable review question or action>
  cta: <one stage-appropriate action>
  claim_ids: <all claim IDs used across body and derivatives>
  limitations: <essential case and capability boundaries>
  body: <final article plus separated derivative text and source note>
  external_action: not_authorized
```

Article body sequence: the consequential decision → what the official record says → the specific broken link → the institution's response and remaining uncertainty → a small reconstructed review artifact → the task the reader can test. Label analysis separately from reported findings. If the source is an advisory, use an advisory structure rather than inventing a historical incident.

Company Page caption sequence: concrete opening → enough context to understand it → why the derivative is useful → essential source qualification → one primary action. No generic AI opener or invented professional anecdote.

Carousel brief sequence: the decision → the obstacle → evidence that changes the reader's understanding → response and limits → useful review question → next action. Frames may be combined or expanded to fit the evidence. Each frame must specify its text, narrative purpose, claim IDs, qualification, source location and visual/alt-text brief. A designer should be able to build it without guessing what the source proves.

A public reconstruction may contain incomplete chronology or no evidence of an approval. Say “not established in the reviewed public sources,” rather than asserting the institution had no approval. The companion never opens customer data or promises a live sharing feature merely because it looks like a product screen.

## Measures and stop rules

Review after four weeks of actual approved distribution, or earlier if evidence shows a material problem. Do not start a clock on fictional publication dates. Initial baselines and outcomes are unknown.

| Measure | Definition and evidence | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Useful work | Accepted drafts that supply a distinct, usable review question and source-linked artifact. | Report acceptance and review time. Production volume alone is not success. |
| Qualified recognition | Actual intended-role reply names a recent task, consequence or material proof gap. | Listener records source, time and unknown fields. General praise stays general praise. |
| Observed sharing | A consented, recorded forward or recipient event with known scope. | Stated intention is separate. No hidden tracking or inference from a click. |
| Qualified sharing rate | Observed qualified forward/recipient events divided by the corresponding measured exposure population, with the event definition stated. | Do not mix forwards and recipients in one numerator. If exposure is unavailable, report counts only and rate as unknown. |
| Private-Trace movement | Actual requests linked to a content/Trace version, then named task, owner, date and approved data-route state. | A request is distinct from a commenced or paid engagement. |
| Commercial movement | Completed fit conversation, named completed tender, proposal or contracting step evidenced in the Operating Room. | Preserve existing IDs and dates. No inference from silence. |
| Claim correction rate | Material claims corrected after release / material claims released during the same observed period. | State numerator/denominator and distinguish pre-publication review revisions. |

Pause a case and its derivatives when primary evidence is unavailable, claim status is disputed, a material correction arrives or the required publication approval is absent/expired. Stop a draft after two failed revisions or its job budget. Supervisor decides whether to narrow it, change evidence or retire it. Never continue production simply to maintain cadence.

For a correction, preserve the original version, record the challenged claim, inspect the primary source, mark dependent assets and invalidate stale approvals. Prepare exact corrected text and an appropriate correction notice for review. External corrections and takedowns follow the required action authority. Librarian updates accepted memory only after the review and decision are recorded.
